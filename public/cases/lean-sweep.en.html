Project overview

A phased improvement program that groups small Kaizen opportunities by scope and priority.

Problem

Waste across several steps disrupted flow and work at the production area.

Current state

  • Excess material
  • Waiting
  • Unnecessary movement
  • Machine and material layout
  • Inventory and handover
  • Difficult-to-see, difficult-to-do tasks
  • Heavy lifting

Root cause

[TO BE ADDED] Root-cause analysis will be added when details are confirmed.

Countermeasures

Group opportunities into 11 workstreams and implement in 3 phases, from flow to point-of-work tasks.

Improvement results

The simulated scenario reduces total waste by 30%, cycle time by 35%, and travel time by 25%.

Sustain

Schedule checks, review improvements, and update operating standards.

Project role

Proposed ideas, designed forms, grouped Kaizen actions, analyzed data, reported progress, and tracked PDCA.

Phased improvement process

Workstreams progress from area flow to standards and tools, then focus on tasks and materials at the point of work.

Selected Kaizen activities in the project

Illustrated as simulated before/after concepts.

↔01 · Machine and material layout

BEFORE · scattered layout

Equipment and materials are far apart, with crossing travel paths.

AFTER · flow-based layout

Work points follow the process sequence, with materials near point of use.

Note: illustrative layout concept to reduce travel and material searching.

⌁02 · Shorter travel path

BEFORE · indirect route

Operators travel between areas to collect materials and tools.

AFTER · points of use closer together

Reposition pickup and return points to shorten the route.

Note: the route diagram illustrates a Kaizen concept.

⚙03 · Multi-purpose tool

BEFORE · separate tools

Different rulers and gauges must be picked up and exchanged during the task.

AFTER · one multi-head tool

Combine measuring and locating features in one multi-purpose tool.

Note: tool drawing is conceptual and requires process-specific validation.

⬆04 · Reduce heavy lifting

BEFORE · manual handling

Boxes are lifted and moved by hand.

AFTER · cart-assisted movement

Use a suitable cart or transfer point to reduce manual lifting.

Note: select handling aids for the load and aisle conditions.

◎05 · Make work visible and simple

BEFORE · hard-to-find tools

Without fixed locations, tools take time to find and put away.

AFTER · visual position markers

Add labels and position marks to make tools easy to identify and return.

Note: visual standards are illustrative for the work area.

✂06 · Reuse fabric offcuts

BEFORE · discarded offcuts

Fabric left over from the cutting layout is not reused.

AFTER · reuse remaining fabric

Revise the cutting layout to make secondary products from offcuts.

Note: cutting layout is a simulated concept and does not show actual output.
