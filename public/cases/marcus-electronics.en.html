Marcus Electronics: E-commerce Platform

An electronics commerce system connecting product catalogues and SKU-level inventory with checkout, payment and administration workflows.

Project context

The project focused on consistent purchasing and operations across the customer storefront and administration area.

Solution design

Designed REST APIs and Vue interfaces around business workflows. Separated customer and administrator access, and organized product, SKU option, price history and inventory data.

Key workflows

  • Product catalogue, search and product details
  • SKU options and inventory tracking
  • Cart, checkout and COD/VNPAY payment flows
  • Order administration and reporting
