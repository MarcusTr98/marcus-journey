Marcus Video: Interactive Video Platform

A video application built with Jakarta Servlet and JSP, supporting content discovery, user interaction, administration and real-time communication.

Java 21Jakarta Servlet 6JSP · JSTLJPA 3.1Hibernate 6.4SQL ServerWebSocketBCrypt
GitHub ↗

Project context

The project focused on layered web architecture, separating request handling, business logic and data access without relying on a full-stack framework.

Solution design

Organized the application into DAO, service and controller layers. Added authentication, content management and interaction features for viewers and administrators.

Key workflows

  • Video discovery and content management
  • Favorites, watch history and sharing
  • Real-time chat with WebSocket
  • BCrypt authentication and email password recovery
  • JSP/JSTL administration dashboard, charts and content management

Role & contribution

Developed the full-stack application, including data and business layers, authentication, user interactions and administration tools.
